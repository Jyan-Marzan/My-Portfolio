Mission

Cavite State University shall provide excellent, equitable, relevant educational opportunity in the arts, science, technology through quality instruction, responsive research, and development activities. it shall produce professional, skilled, and morally upright individuals for globally competitiveness.

Vision

The premier university in historic Cavite globally recognized for excellence in character development, academic, research, innovation, and sustainable community engagement.

Quality Policy

We commit to the highest standards of education, value our stakeholders, Strive for continual improvement of our products and services, and Uphold the University's tenet of Truth, Excellence, and Service ton produce globally competitive and morally upright individuals.
